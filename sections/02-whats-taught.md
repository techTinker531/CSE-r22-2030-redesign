# Section 2 — What's Being Taught

All 8 semesters mapped, including electives taken. Full R22 vs R25 structural diff completed and verified against official sources.

## Key structural findings

- **ML/Statistics sequencing regression:** R22 kept Statistics and Machine Learning 3 semesters apart, preserving the prerequisite chain. R25 moved them into the *same* semester, breaking that sequencing.
- **Compiler Design + Formal Languages merged in R25:** was 2 subjects × 3 credits across 2 semesters → now 1 subject × 3 credits. 3 credits freed up as a result (used as the ADD-item funding source in Section 6).
- **DevOps credits unchanged in R25** (3+1 credits, III-I) — despite the HKAIVERSE brief's claim of a cut (see Section 8).
- **Two open electives are cross-branch, non-CS subjects:** Renewable Energy Sources (Mechanical), Remote Sensing & GIS (Civil) — neither deepens CS skills.
- **Cloud Computing exists as a Professional Elective (PE IV)** in R22 — not a zero-coverage gap, just not core.
